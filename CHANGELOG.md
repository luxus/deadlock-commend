## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

First public release.

### Added

- Four post-game buttons on the Team, Scoreboard and MVP screens: Team, Enemy, All, and Return.
- A "Commend" tab in [CompLock HUD++](https://deadlockconfig.com/), or an ADDONS window in the Esc menu without HUD++.
- One "Commend buttons" toggle (on by default). "Reset Commend" on the right of the settings foot restores it.
- Toggle rows match HUD++: a compact line with the description as a tooltip on hover.
- Addon Settings library 1.1.3 (BUILD 5), vendored from Improved Quickbuy.
